Weekly Workout Plan

Monday: Push Day + SwimMorning: Strength (Chest & Triceps)
1. Bench Press: 5x4-6 reps (65 lbs DBs; increase weekly). 55 in barbell
2. Incline Dumbbell Press: 4x8-10 reps (50 lbs DBs). 10-12 reps with 20 kgs in each side
3. Weighted Dips: 4x10-12 reps.
4. Close-Grip Push-Ups: 3 sets to failure.
5. Overhead Tricep Extensions: 3x10-12 reps.
Evening: Swimming (Endurance)
1. Warm-Up: 200m easy swim.
2. Main Set: 8x100m at race pace (track total time).
3. Cool Down: 200m easy swim.

Tuesday: Pull Day + MMA/BoxingMorning: Strength (Back & Biceps)
1. Deadlifts: 5x4-6 reps.
2. Pull-Ups: 4x8-10 reps (add weight if possible).
3. Bent-Over Rows: 4x8-10 reps (45 lbs + 25).
4. Hammer Curls: 4x10-12 reps (14 kg DBs).
5. Lat Pulldowns: 3x10-12 reps (50 kg per side).
* 1 chin-ups/narrow grip pull, 
* 2 narrow grip pulldown.
Evening: MMA/Boxing
* Technique drills, footwork, sparring.

Wednesday: Interval Run + SwimMorning: Interval Run
1. Warm-Up: 10 mins easy jog.
2. 8x400m at max effort, 90s rest.
3. Cool Down: 10 mins easy jog.
Evening: Swimming (Recovery)
1. Warm-Up: 200m easy swim.
2. Main Set: 6x200m at moderate pace.
3. Cool Down: 200m easy swim.

Thursday: Leg Day + RowingMorning: Strength (Legs)
1. Squats: 5x4-6 reps (25 kg per side).
2. Walking Lunges: 4x10-12 reps/leg (20 lbs kettlebells).
3. Romanian Deadlifts: 4x8-10 reps (20 kg per side).
4. Bulgarian Split Squats: 4x10-12 reps/leg (20 lbs kettlebells).
5. Calf Raises: 3x20 reps.
Evening: Rowing Cardio
1. Warm-Up: 5 mins light rowing.
2. 5x (1 min hard effort, 1 min recovery).
3. Cool Down: 5 mins light rowing.

Friday: Push Day + SwimMorning: Strength (Shoulders & Abs)
1. Overhead Press: 5x4-6 reps (20 kg DBs). 45 pounds 10 reps
2. Lateral Raises: 4x12-15 reps (10-12 kg DBs).
3. Front Raises: 3x10-12 reps.
4. Weighted Russian Twists: 4x20 reps.
Evening: Swimming (Intervals)
1. Warm-Up: 200m easy swim.
2. Main Set: 8x100m at race pace.
3. Cool Down: 200m easy swim.

Saturday: MMA/Boxing + Pull DayMorning: MMA/Boxing
* Sparring, bag work, conditioning.
Evening: Strength (Back & Biceps)
1. Pull-Ups: 4x8-10 reps.
2. Bent-Over Rows: 4x10-12 reps (60 lbs).
3. Bicep Curls: 4x10-12 reps (25 lbs DBs).
4. Lat Pulldowns: 4x10-12 reps (70 lbs).

Sunday: Long Run + RecoveryMorning: Long Run
* 15-18 km at Zone 2 heart rate.
Evening: Recovery
* Yoga or mobility-focused stretching (45-60 mins).
